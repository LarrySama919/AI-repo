Privacy overview

Your information, explained clearly.

A plain-language overview of how this Skinwise prototype handles details you enter, photos you choose, and reports you save.

Prototype overview · Updated November 24, 2026

On this page

This prototype overview is not a substitute for a reviewed privacy notice.

i

Please read before sharing personal information. The site is a prototype. Its public-facing data handling, retention periods, and support channels have not been fully documented here. Avoid uploading sensitive or identifying photos until those details are confirmed.

01

Information you provide

If you create an account, the sign-up page requests your name, email address, and password. If you use a scan feature, the selected image is sent to the existing analysis service to return informational results.

Saved scan reports are stored in your browser on your device. Your browser may also store a sign-in token so the account experience can remember your session.

02

How information is used

The current experience uses account details to provide sign-in and account pages, and uses an uploaded image to prepare the requested analysis response. Saved reports support the history and progress views in the browser.

Do not upload a photo of another person unless you have their permission. Avoid including faces, documents, or other details that identify someone when they are not needed for your check-in.

03

Storage and retention

Some information is handled by the account and analysis services; saved reports are kept in local browser storage. Clearing the browser’s site data may remove locally saved reports or sign-in details.

This prototype does not publish a complete server-side retention schedule or self-service data export and deletion process. Those details should be confirmed before the service is used publicly.

04

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account or upload a photo. You can clear local site data through your browser settings, though that does not delete information held by connected services.

If you have questions about account or image handling, use the Contact page for the current prototype status. No public support email or connected message-delivery service is configured at this time.

05

Policy updates

This overview may change as the prototype evolves. Before a public launch, the service owner should publish a reviewed privacy notice that reflects the production services, vendors, retention practices, and applicable requirements.
